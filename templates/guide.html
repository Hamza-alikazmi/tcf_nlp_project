How to Choose
Your Stream

A Step-by-Step Guide to Picking the Right Subjects

01

Check Your Interest

Ask yourself which subjects you enjoy most. Studying something you like makes learning easier and fun. (e.g., Biology vs Maths vs Economics).

02

Your Future Goal

Do you dream of becoming a doctor, engineer, or designer? Knowing your goal helps you pick the right subjects now.

03

Check Your Strengths

  • ➤ Math/Physics = Science
  • ➤ Numbers/Business = Commerce
  • ➤ Arts/Creativity = Humanities
