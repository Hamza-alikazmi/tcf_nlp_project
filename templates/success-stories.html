"One day, as a kid, you're wondering if you will ever go to school. Next thing you know, you are packing your bags to go to Harvard!"

Coming from Orangi – a small town, a slum – women like me are told places like Harvard aren't for us. Getting in was beyond what my parents or I ever imagined. We celebrate Kainat's grit and your belief in the potential of Pakistan's children.
