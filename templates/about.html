Welcome to
TCF Alumni Pathways

A dedicated department of The Citizen Foundation (TCF), established in 2005 with our head office in Karachi and regional branches in Islamabad and Lahore. Our mission is to guide TCF graduates as they transition from school to higher education and professional careers.

What We Do

We provide comprehensive support to TCF graduates in several critical areas:

Higher Education Guidance

Advising students on fields of study, career scope, and selecting colleges nationwide.

University Applications

Helping with admission procedures, timelines, and rigorous document preparation.

Financial Support (Qarza-e-Hasana)

Assisting deserving students with tuition, hostel fees, and transport costs.

Technical & Career Training

Dedicated mentorship for those pursuing technical or vocational pathways.

How We Work

Students apply for financial assistance through Qarza-e-Hasana. Each application undergoes a careful review and interview process to assess eligibility.

Approved students receive tailored financial support covering:

  • + Tuition Fees
  • + Hostel & Mess Fees
  • + Transport Costs
  • + Educational Supplies

Impact & Success

Since 2005, TCF Alumni Pathways has guided over 5,000 students and provided financial support to more than 2,000 graduates.
