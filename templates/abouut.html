Our Programs

What We Do

Comprehensive support for TCF graduates at every milestone — from classroom to career

01
Higher Education Guidance

Advising students on fields of study, career scope, and colleges beyond their own cities.

02
College & University Applications

Navigating admission procedures, timelines, ensuring no student misses opportunities.

03
Financial Support — Qarza-e-Hasana

Tailored funding for tuition, hostel, transport, covering actual student needs.

04
Ongoing Mentorship

Progress reports and advisor check-ins from admission through graduation.

05
Technical & Vocational Training

Same mentorship and financial assistance for technical & vocational students.

06
Career Development & Placement

Bridging graduation to professional life with career opportunities matching ambitions.

Our Process

How We Work

A structured, human-centered process ensures every applicant receives honest assessment, and approved students receive consistent support.

01
Application Submission
Apply for Qarza-e-Hasana through local alumni office.
02
Careful Review
Individual evaluation and shortlisting for interview.
03
Eligibility Interview
In-depth conversation about needs and academic goals.
04
Tailored Financial Support
Calibrated assistance based on actual requirements.
05
Progress Monitoring
Regular check-ins until graduation day.

Support Covers

Tuition Fees
Full or partial coverage based on assessment
Hostel & Mess Fees
Accommodation for students away from home
Transport Costs
Daily commute support where required
